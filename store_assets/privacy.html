Privacy Policy — Wantok Radio Light app

Introduction

Wantok Radio Light ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and disclose your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

Our app does not require you to create an account. We only receive the details you choose to type into a Prayer Request or Song Request form (your name, phone number, country, location and message). These are sent to our studio team so we can respond on air or in prayer, and are not used for any other purpose.

Permissions

The app requires certain permissions to function:
• Internet: To stream radio content and send your requests.
• Background audio / Foreground Service: To keep the radio playing while you use other apps or when the screen is off.
• Notifications: To provide playback controls in the notification area and lock screen, to announce our monthly News Letter, and — only if you turn them on — to deliver the daily devotionals at 5:00 am and 9:00 pm.

Monthly News Letter

At the end of each month the app shows the station's News Letter. To announce it, the app uses Google Firebase Cloud Messaging, which gives your phone a random notification ID. It is not linked to your name, phone number or email, and is not used for advertising. You can turn notifications off at any time in your phone's Settings; the News Letter will still appear inside the app.

Daily Devotional Notifications

If you turn on devotional reminders, the app uses Google Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver them. Firebase gives your phone a random notification ID so the reminders can reach it, and the app shares your phone's time zone (for example "Pacific/Port_Moresby") so they arrive at 5:00 am and 9:00 pm your local time. Neither is linked to your name or phone number, and neither is used for advertising. You can turn the reminders off at any time on the Devotional page or in your phone's Settings.

Third-Party Services

We use Google Sheets (via Google Apps Script) to receive requests, Google Firebase to store the devotionals and send reminders, and our streaming provider to deliver the live broadcast and "now playing" information. These services may process information as governed by their own privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Data Sharing

We do not collect, sell, or trade your personal information.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at wrlstudio@wantokradio.org
