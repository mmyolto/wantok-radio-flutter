## Full description
Wantok Radio Light — Your Inspiration Station — brings PNG Christian Broadcasting Network to your phone and tablet, wherever you are.

• Listen live with one tap, in high quality
• Keeps playing in the background and with the screen off
• Control playback from the lock screen, notification, headphones, Bluetooth and your car
• See the song that's playing now
• Sleep timer to stop the radio automatically
• Weekday program schedule with what's on air now
• Send prayer requests and song dedications straight to the studio
• Contact the studio by SMS, WhatsApp, e-mail or phone
• Support the ministry by bank transfer
• Reconnects automatically if your connection drops

Wantok Radio Light preaches JESUS CHRIST to Papua New Guineans in towns and remote communities, and to listeners around the world. Also on 105.9 FM, 93.9 FM Port Moresby and 105.7 FM Goroka, and online at wantokradio.org.

## Google Play — Data safety answers
- Data collected: **Name**, **Phone number**, **Other user-generated content** (prayer / song request text), **Approximate location** only as typed by the user ("where you're listening from").
- Collected only when the user sends a request; purpose: **App functionality** (the studio responds to the request).
- Data is **not shared** with third parties, **not used for advertising**, encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
- Users can request deletion by e-mailing wrlstudio@wantokradio.org.
- No account, no tracking, no ads.
- **Device or other IDs** (Firebase Cloud Messaging ID) — collected only when the listener turns on devotional reminders; purpose **App functionality**; not shared; not linked to the request details.
- The phone's **time zone name** (e.g. Pacific/Port_Moresby) is sent so reminders arrive at local time. It is stored on its own, not linked to any person or device, and is not location data in Play's sense (no GPS, no address).

## Apple — App Privacy answers
- Data types: **Name**, **Phone Number**, **Other User Content**. For each: purpose **App Functionality**, linked to the user's identity **No**, used for tracking **No**.
- **Device ID** (Firebase Cloud Messaging, for the optional devotional reminders): purpose **App Functionality**, linked to identity **No**, tracking **No**.
- Background modes: Audio (live radio), Remote notifications (devotional reminders).

## Apple — Review notes (paste into "Notes" in App Store Connect)
This is the official live radio app of Wantok Radio Light (PNG Christian Broadcasting Network). Tap Play on the first screen to hear the live stream; it continues in the background (UIBackgroundModes: audio). No login is needed. The Devotional page shows short devotionals written by the station's Radio Chaplain; listeners can choose to get them as notifications at 5:00 am and 9:00 pm in their own local time. The Donation screen only shows the station's bank details for transfers made outside the app; no payments are taken in the app.
